## 🎨 Dedicated Interface Mood Boards

### 1️⃣ Faculty Dashboard Mood Board
* **Objective**: Provide lecture instructors with real-time dwell surveillance, 45-minute progression dials, proxy exception resolution, and continuous evaluation (CE) scoring.
* **Key Visual Elements**:
  * Glowing `#BCFF00` radial time-remaining dials and lecture progress bars.
  * Real-time student attendance matrix with status indicators: **Present ($\ge 45\text{m}$)**, **Review Flag ($30\text{--}44\text{m}$)**, and **Absent ($< 30\text{m}$)**.
  * Instant proxy buddy-punch exception triage panel with 1-click approve/flag action.
  * Continuous Evaluation (CE) rubric gradebook for internal assessments.

---

### 2️⃣ Student Attendance & Biometric Privacy Portal Mood Board
* **Objective**: Offer students total transparency into their dwell accumulation, attendance percentages, and biometric privacy assurances.
* **Key Visual Elements**:
  * Circular attendance percentage rings with color threshold coding ($\ge 75\%$ green standing).
  * Minute-by-minute session dwell timeline breakdown showing doorway check-in timestamp and classroom dwell duration.
  * 1-Click dispute ledger allowing students to submit attendance appeals within 24 hours.
  * Ephemeral Privacy Badge confirming raw camera vectors have been purged from volatile RAM.

---

### 3️⃣ Administrator Telemetry, Timetable & 12h Audit Hub Mood Board
* **Objective**: Central command interface for campus IT administrators to monitor edge device health, Micro-UPS battery levels, and verify compliance with the 12-hour zero retention protocol.
* **Key Visual Elements**:
  * Edge gateway CPU, RAM, and thermal load curve analytics.
  * Micro-UPS power mode monitor displaying battery charge ($100\%$) and failover status ($<4\text{ms}$).
  * Local SQLite WAL offline buffering queue monitor ($0\text{ pending synced}$).
  * Cryptographic 12-Hour Zero Retention audit trail log.

---

### 4️⃣ AI Camera & Multi-Person Monitoring Station Mood Board
* **Objective**: Real-time AI computer vision processing HUD for classroom presence and engagement analytics.
* **Key Visual Elements**:
  * Glowing neon lime `#BCFF00` multi-face bounding reticles with confidence percentage metrics.
  * Real-time spatial engagement heatmap visualizing classroom seat density and attention hot zones.
  * Doorway 13.56MHz RFID / NFC synchronization feed.
  * Volatile in-memory tensor processing pipeline monitor.

---

### 5️⃣ Mobile Phone Connect Node Mood Board
* **Objective**: Enable smartphones to operate as low-latency wireless camera nodes and portable doorway check-in terminals for pop-up or temporary classrooms.
* **Key Visual Elements**:
  * Low-latency 1080p @ 30 FPS WebRTC wireless camera streaming node ($\sim 85\text{ms}$ latency).
  * Built-in smartphone NFC sensor integration for doorway badge scanning.
  * Client-side battery optimization with thermal throttle protection ($\sim 12\%/\text{hr}$).

---

### 6️⃣ External Hardware Devices Mood Board
* **Objective**: Physical hardware module deployment specifications and wiring interconnects.
* **Module Breakdown**:
  1. **Doorway Dual-Factor 2FA Scanner**:
     * ISO14443A 13.56MHz NFC/RFID badge reader.
     * 850nm Near-Infrared (NIR) optical sensor array for instantaneous anti-spoofing facial recognition ($<300\text{ms}$).
  2. **Single Overhead AI Classroom Camera**:
     * 4K UHD 120-degree wide-angle lens with low-light NIR sensitivity.
     * Ceiling-mounted at room center covering 40+ student seating capacity without blind spots.
  3. **Edge AI Gateway Processor**:
     * Rugged aluminum fanless chassis with NPU tensor accelerator.
     * Integrated Micro-UPS battery pack delivering up to 4.8 hours of autonomous operation during power outages.
     * Volatile RAM frame shredder enforcing zero persistent biometric footage storage.
  4. **SQLite WAL Local Offline Sync Engine**:
     * High-speed Write-Ahead Logging local database guaranteeing zero transaction drops during network downtime with automatic cloud flush.
